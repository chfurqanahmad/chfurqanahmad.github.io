The client and the opportunity

SokolDev provides web and mobile development, UI/UX design, AI and workflow automation, staff augmentation, SEO and lead-generation services. My work supported the commercial side: identifying relevant businesses, qualifying prospects and helping the team start client conversations.

My contribution

I researched prospective clients and decision-makers, assessed their relevance to the service being offered, and delivered leads with qualification context. Research covered potential buyers of IT, software, design, research, lead-generation and outreach support.

I supported the path from research to meetings and tailored service proposals. Alongside this work, I contributed to R&D, analyst training, workflow creation, quality maintenance and strategy.

Results and deliverables

More than 1,000 prospects researched and qualified across more than 500 businesses.

More than 80 client meetings earned, with leads and research supporting the team’s business-development activity.

Qualification context, lead handoffs and tailored service approaches that helped the team prepare relevant conversations.

What this means for your team

I can take on prospect research and qualification, deliver usable leads, and support outreach and interested-reply handoffs. The starting point is your service, target buyer and a clear definition of fit.
