My contribution

I researched companies and prospects, assessed relevance and supplied leads to the team. I also helped turn interested conversations into tailored service approaches, one-page breakdowns and proposals.

My wider SokolDev responsibilities included research and R&D, analyst training, workflow creation, quality maintenance and strategy. This connected day-to-day prospecting with more consistent research and delivery practices.

What the work delivered

Researched prospects and lead lists for business-development activity.

Meetings earned through the team’s outreach work, supported by research and follow-through.

Service approaches and proposal support that connected a prospect’s needs with a practical scope of work.

Research workflows, quality review and training support for the wider team.

How this helps your business

If your team can deliver its service but needs help identifying suitable companies and preparing relevant conversations, I can support the research, lead preparation and follow-through.

The scope can start with a focused research brief and sample list, then extend to outreach coordination, reply handling and proposal support as needed.
