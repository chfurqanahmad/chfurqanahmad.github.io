The brief

A fictional employer wanted a partner for digital-skills development in Pakistan. The assessment compared DigiSkills, Saylani Mass IT Training and the Punjab Skills Development Fund using official public materials. It was a demonstration study, not paid work for these providers.

The method

Compare accessibility, technical depth, employer alignment, delivery flexibility, public transparency and evidence of scale. Record information gaps alongside the apparent strengths.

The illustrative weights were 20%, 20%, 20%, 15%, 15% and 10%, respectively. Each provider received a judgment score from one to five. Weighted total = sum of weight multiplied by score divided by five.

The decision

DigiSkills was the proposed starting point for broad, low-cost online access and foundational digital or freelancing skills. SMIT was the alternative for technically intensive pathways such as software development.

PSDF was the proposed starting point for an employer-designed workforce program in Punjab with multiple delivery modes. These are scenario-specific recommendations from the historical desk assessment.

The original weighted matrix favored PSDF at 89/100, followed by DigiSkills at 81 and SMIT at 72. Choosing DigiSkills for a narrower online-reach brief is a separate scenario judgment; it does not mean DigiSkills won the default weighted scorecard.

What needed verification before a real partnership

Current program availability and capacity; completion and employment outcomes; delivery geography; employer responsibilities; pricing or funding terms; and learner data handling.

Official descriptions establish how an organization presents its services. They do not independently establish impact, employment quality or the results a new employer partnership would achieve.

Deliverables and limits

A provider comparison, explicit scoring matrix, scenario-based recommendation and follow-up questions. The useful output is a shortlist with reasons and verification needs.

Scores are illustrative analytical judgments, not audited provider ratings. Sources were assessed in July 2026; program details may have changed. No provider engagement or employment outcome is claimed.
