Visiting this site

This is a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. The site code does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, cookies or visitor accounts. The hosting provider may process connection information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the site.

Project enquiries

When you submit an enquiry, your name, company, email address, subject and goal are sent to a Google-hosted service and saved in a private Google Sheet for reviewing and responding to your request. A submission reference helps avoid duplicate entries when you retry. The form does not save your details in browser storage.

Your enquiry is used to discuss your requirements and a possible scope of work. Please do not include patient information, private client records or other sensitive material in your first enquiry. Email me if you want to request deletion or correction of information you have shared.
